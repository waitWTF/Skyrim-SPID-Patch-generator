Skyrim SPID Patch Generator v0.4.10

Extract the complete folder. Start SkyrimSPIDPatchGenerator.exe, keeping Runtime and Documentation beside it.
Requires .NET 10 Desktop Runtime x64. In MO2, add the top-level EXE as a tool and launch through MO2.
Search an outfit, right-click Configure this outfit, then Save & close or Review & generate.
For invisible body parts, build/deploy the outfit meshes and rescan; see Documentation/README.md.
Existing WearableDistributor output filenames/settings are retained. Experimental outputs are separate.
Source: https://github.com/waitWTF/Skyrim-SPID-Patch-generator

For matching colors and normal/torn alternatives, open an existing setup and choose Balanced. Review & generate updates the installed patch; replacing the app alone does not rewrite it.
